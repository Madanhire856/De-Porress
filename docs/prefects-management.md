# Prefects management

Use Academics → Nominate prefects to submit a student and suggested post with a reason. The separate **Nominate Prefects** group permission is required (system administrators have it automatically). Teacher nominations are restricted to actively enrolled students in the teacher's current assigned classes; the teacher must be linked to a Staff record. Teachers can track their own submissions under Prefect nominations. Duplicate pending nominations for the same student/post in the current year are rejected. Prefects are student records; appointment never creates login accounts or grants system permissions.

Admin, Head and Deputy Head accounts use Prefect nominations to review submissions. ViewPrefects permits viewing, EditPrefects permits marking under review or rejecting with a reason, and CreatePrefects permits appointment. Review and appoint opens the appointment form with the nominee and suggested post. Enter the appointment dates and save. Appointment creation, reviewer metadata and the nomination's appointed status are saved in one transaction. A repeated approval returns the existing appointment rather than creating another.

Direct appointments remain available under Prefects. CreatePrefects and EditPrefects are enforced on GET and POST and restricted to administration roles. Posted IDs cannot switch a create form into editing. Students cannot be changed on existing appointments. Valid dates and enum values, existing nominators and student enrolment are checked. A serializable transaction prevents concurrent overlapping active appointments for a student. Inactive, completed or demoted records do not block a new active appointment.

The list opens appointment details, and the board and list distinguish currently active, upcoming and expired appointments. Expired active records display as completed without rewriting their stored history. Change an appointment's status through Edit; past appointments are retained. Native date inputs work on mobile. Forms contain no Cancel buttons.

The PrefectNominationReview migration adds the appointment link, reviewer, review date and review notes to existing nomination records. Public pages never expose nominations. Approval and rejection are human decisions; this feature does not select or rank students automatically. SeparateNominationPermission moves nomination permission to UserGroup.CanNominatePrefects, removing the legacy collision with ViewAllBursarsCashUp at bit 59. Existing cash-up bit values are retained; legacy nomination permission is copied for groups currently assigned to academic staff roles.
